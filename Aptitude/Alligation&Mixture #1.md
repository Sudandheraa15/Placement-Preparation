Removed And Replace

If removed and replaced value are same use the "formula",
Final Quantity=Initial Quantity(1-(x/c))^t

When to use "Traditional Method" the removed and replaced values are different we can use this method.

1.A vessel contains 80 liters of ethanol 20 liters of this liquid is removed and replaced with water. 
20 liters of this mixture is again removed and replaced with water. How much water is present in th vessel.

Solution:
#Method 1: Traditional method
         80 liter(e)
(-20litre) /     \(+20 litre)
       60(e)     20(w)

ratio of ethanol:water = 60:20 = 3:1
then again removed 20 litre of mixture, but don't know the how many litre of ethanol and water in that 20 liter 
to find that,
        20(e?/w?)
        /   \
  (3/4)*20  (1/4)*20
      |        |
    15(e)     5(w)

The above we used (3/4)*20 and (1/4)*20
because the ratio of ethanol is 3 and total part is 4 that's why (3/4) 
and multiply by 20 because in the removed part only we have find the litres of ethanol

Now we have to find the how much water is persent in the vessel
In first we have added 20 litres of water and then removed 5 litres of water
so Water = 20 - 5
         = 15
and then we repeated that process so we added another 20 litre of water,
Then Water = 15 + 20
           = 35 litres


#Method 2: Formula
Final Quantity = Initial Quantity*(1-(x/c))^t
where Initial Quantity = 80(e),
x=20,
c=80,
t=2;

aplying in formula,
Final Quantity = 80 * (1-(20/80))^2
               = 80 * (1-(1/4))^2
               = 80 * (3/4)^2
               = 80 * (9/16)
               = 5 * 9
               = 45(e)
But we have to find water content so,
total = ethanol + water
water = total - ethanol
      = 80 - 45
      = 35 litres
