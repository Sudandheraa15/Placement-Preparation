#Basic Formula
          Percentage = (obtained value)/(Total Value)) * 100

#Example: If you scored 40 out of 100. find the percentage
#Solution
         Percentage = 40/100 * 100
                    = 4/10 * 100
                    = 400/10
                    =40%
